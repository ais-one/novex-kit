# Minerva Inc. — Refund & Return Policy

## Overview

At Minerva Inc., we want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you are not satisfied, we offer refunds and returns under the following conditions.

---

## Refund Eligibility

### Standard Products (Non-Customized)
- **Refund Window**: 14 days from date of delivery
- **Condition**: Item must be unused, unwashed, and in original packaging with all tags attached
- **Refund Amount**: **80% of the original purchase price**
- **Processing Time**: Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after we receive the returned item

### Customized Products (e.g., Fully Customized Cup)
- **Refund Window**: 7 days from date of delivery
- **Condition**: Item must be unused and in original packaging
- **Refund Amount**: **80% of the original purchase price** (same rate as standard products)
- **Note**: Since customized items are made specifically for you, we encourage reviewing your customization details carefully before placing your order

### Damaged or Defective Items
- **Refund Window**: 30 days from date of delivery
- **Condition**: Item arrived damaged, defective, or significantly different from description
- **Refund Amount**: **100% of the original purchase price** (full refund)
- **Required**: Photo evidence of damage/defect must be provided via email to merch@minervainc.id

---

## Refund Calculation

All refunds are calculated at **80% of the original purchase price**.

### Examples:

| Product | Original Price | Refund Amount (80%) |
|---------|---------------|-------------------|
| Jacket Bomber | $80.00 | **$64.00** |
| Fully Customized Cup | $5.00 | **$4.00** |
| T-Shirt Classic | $25.00 | **$20.00** |
| Hoodie | $55.00 | **$44.00** |
| Cap | $18.00 | **$14.40** |
| Sticker Pack | $3.00 | **$2.40** |

**Note**: Shipping costs are non-refundable. The refund applies only to the product price.

## How to Request a Refund

### Step 1: Contact Customer Service
- **WhatsApp**: +62 812-3456-7890
- **Email**: merch@minervainc.id
- **Subject Line**: "Refund Request — [Order Number]"

### Step 2: Provide Required Information
When contacting us, please provide:
1. Your full name
2. Order number (format: MNV-YYYYMMDD-XXXX)
3. Email address used for the order
4. Phone number
5. Product(s) you wish to refund
6. Reason for refund

### Step 3: Receive Return Authorization
Our team will review your request and provide a Return Authorization Number (RA#) within 1–2 business days.

### Step 4: Ship the Item Back
- Pack the item securely in its original packaging
- Include the RA# on the outside of the package
- Ship to: **Minerva Inc. Returns Center, Jl. Kemang Raya No. 88, Jakarta Selatan 12730, Indonesia**
- Customer is responsible for return shipping costs

### Step 5: Receive Your Refund
- Once we receive and inspect the item, we will process your refund
- Refund will be issued to your original payment method
- Processing time: 5–7 business days after item receipt

---

## Refund Confirmation

When a refund request is successfully submitted, you will receive:
1. **Refund Confirmation PDF** — A formal document containing:
   - Refund reference number
   - Product details and refund amount
   - Expected processing timeline
   - Contact information for follow-up
2. **Email confirmation** sent to your registered email address
3. **WhatsApp notification** with refund status updates

---

## Exchanges

We currently do not offer direct exchanges. If you would like a different product or size, please:
1. Return the original item for a refund (80% of original price)
2. Place a new order for the desired item

---

## Non-Refundable Items

The following items are **not eligible** for refund:
- Items returned after the refund window (14 days for standard, 7 days for customized)
- Items that have been used, washed, or altered
- Items without original packaging or tags
- Gift cards and digital products
- Items purchased during clearance sales (marked as "Final Sale")

---

## Cancellation Policy

- **Before Shipping**: Full refund (100%) if order is cancelled before it ships
- **After Shipping**: Standard refund policy applies (80% of original price)
- **Customized Items**: Cannot be cancelled once production has started (within 2 hours of order placement)
