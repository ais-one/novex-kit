# Minerva Inc. — Company Information

## About Us

Minerva Inc. is an entertainment and media company headquartered in Jakarta, Indonesia. Founded in 2019, we have grown to become one of Indonesia's leading YouTube content creation studios, specializing in Minecraft gameplay, tutorials, and entertainment content.

## Our Mission

To create fun, engaging, and family-friendly Minecraft content that brings joy to millions of viewers across Indonesia and Southeast Asia. We believe in the power of gaming to inspire creativity, build community, and provide wholesome entertainment for all ages.

## What We Do

### YouTube Content Creation
- **Main Channel**: Minerva Gaming (2.5M+ subscribers)
- **Content Types**: Minecraft survival series, creative builds, mod reviews, challenge videos, and collaborative events
- **Language**: Primarily Bahasa Indonesia with English subtitles on select videos
- **Upload Schedule**: New videos every Tuesday, Thursday, and Saturday at 19:00 WIB (Western Indonesian Time)
- **Average Views**: 500K–2M views per video

### Live Streaming
- Weekly live streams on YouTube every Friday at 20:00 WIB
- Interactive Minecraft sessions with viewers
- Special event streams during holidays and milestones

### Merchandise
- Official Minerva Inc. merchandise available through our online store
- Products include apparel, accessories, and custom items
- Ships nationwide across Indonesia and internationally to select countries
